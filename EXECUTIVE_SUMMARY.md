# Testimonial Uploader — Executive Summary

## The Problem

Collecting video testimonials from course graduates is a manual, time-consuming process. It typically involves back-and-forth emails, file sharing links, incompatible video formats, and hours of administrative work to organize and transcribe the results. Many testimonials are lost simply because the process is too cumbersome for students to complete.

## The Solution

**Testimonial Uploader** is a simple, self-service web application where customers can record and submit a video testimonial in under 3 minutes — directly from their browser, with no software to install.

A student visits the page, types their name and course, takes a quick headshot photo, records a short video (up to 2 minutes), and clicks Submit. That's it.

## What Happens Behind the Scenes

- **Headshot and video** are automatically uploaded to a shared Google Drive folder, organized by student name and course.
- **A written transcript** of the video is generated automatically using Google's Gemini AI, saving hours of manual transcription.
- **All submissions** are cataloged in a database and viewable through a private admin dashboard.

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Zero friction for students** | No app downloads, no file sharing — just a web link |
| **Automatic organization** | Every submission gets its own Drive folder with files named consistently |
| **Instant transcripts** | AI-generated transcripts are ready for review within minutes |
| **Admin visibility** | Browse all submissions, watch videos, and read transcripts from one dashboard |
| **Secure and private** | Admin access is password-protected; files are stored in your own Google Drive |
| **Low cost to operate** | Runs on Google Cloud with minimal infrastructure costs |

## Who Is This For?

Any education business, coaching program, or course creator who wants to collect authentic video testimonials from their students without the back-and-forth of manual collection.
